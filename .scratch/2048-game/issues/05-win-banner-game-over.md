**Blocked by:** 04 — Score, Best Score, New Game, and Best Score persistence

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] A "You Win" banner appears the first time a 2048 Tile appears on the Grid

- [ ] The Win banner does not reappear later in the same game once shown
- [ ] A "Game Over" overlay appears when the Grid is full and no Move would produce a Merge in any direction
